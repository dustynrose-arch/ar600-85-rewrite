Official U.S. Department of the Army emblem (not the authentication seal).
Unmodified raster of the Institute of Heraldry artwork.

Source: Wikimedia Commons

Authoritative origin: U.S. Army Institute of Heraldry (public-domain U.S. government work).
This file is the official emblem, which AR 840-1 authorizes for official / quasi-official
display. The Department of the Army seal (War Office / authentication instrument) is
intentionally not used so this working-group tool cannot be mistaken for an
AR 25-30 authenticated publication.

Do not recolor, crop, or otherwise alter the mark.
Always show this file paired with the gold DRAFT / WORKING COPY banner
(and the TRAINING banner in Training). Never show seals alone.
